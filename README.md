# Semantic image segmentation of road scenes

MSc project, Royal Holloway, University of London (July to August 2023).

A comparative study of semantic segmentation for road images: U-Net, Mask R-CNN and DeepLabv3 trained and evaluated on a curated road-scene dataset, with transfer learning from ImageNet backbones, and results reported on IoU, pixel accuracy, precision, recall and F1. The models were also tested on unseen road images from other geographies and lighting conditions to check how well they carried over.

The full write-up, including the dataset description, training setup, results tables and failure analysis, is in the report in this repository:

- [project_report 2.pdf](./project_report%202.pdf)

Tools: TensorFlow, PyTorch, OpenCV, NumPy.
